## Question

Do observed customer behaviour, vendor participation, availability accuracy and operating effort justify the next bounded investment in ThelaRadar?

## Evidence available

- [Competitor desk research](../research/competitors.md) shows published feature overlap; it does not establish local demand or competitor performance.

- [Interview materials](../research/interview-guide.md), [provisional criteria](../validation/pilot-plan.md) and [manual workflow](../validation/manual-pilot.md) are prepared.

No field interviews, actual participant commitments, pilot observations, reliable revenue or measured maintenance costs have been recorded in the project. Absence of recorded evidence is not proof that demand is absent.

Use the frozen pilot plan's definitions and report raw counts, denominators and missing observations. Do not replace field evidence with screenshots, registrations, positive comments or hypothetical willingness to pay.

## Options for the later review

1. Continue: commission a bounded MVP or another specific experiment when evidence supports it. State scope, owner, cost/time ceiling and success criteria; passing a small pilot does not justify statewide expansion.
2. Revise: change the audience, locality, update process or scope when demand exists but the proposed operation fails. Record the hypothesis and version the next experiment before running it.
3. Stop or pause investment: when evidence indicates low recurring value or an operating burden that cannot be justified. Preserve findings and the original vision.
4. Inconclusive: obtain targeted missing evidence where recruitment, observation or follow-up is inadequate. Name the missing evidence and the next action.

No option is selected on behalf of the founder merely by preparing this record. Current evidence supports continuing preparation and discovery, not declaring market validation.
