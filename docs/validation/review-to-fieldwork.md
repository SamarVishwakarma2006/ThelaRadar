## Minimal daily record

Use field-rehearsal.csv as an empty schema. Copy into ignored private/ for real records. Use participant codes rather than names/phone numbers. One row is one event; do not fabricate zeros for missing responses.

Events: planned_session, vendor_update, audit, visit, operator_work, refusal, withdrawal.
Prompt: none, group_reminder, individual_chase, onboarding_demo, unknown.
Outcome: bought_requested_item, item_missing, vendor_absent, correct, incorrect, unknown, not_applicable.

Record event time and confirmation expiry in ISO format with +05:30 offset. Operator minutes include reminders, verification, moderation and corrections; record shared work once. Onboarding time is separate. Optional visits are participant reports, not proven incremental sales. Record reasons for missing/unknown evidence.

## End-of-week questions

1. Unprompted updated planned sessions / all planned sessions, by vendor. Exclude onboarding demos from spontaneous updates; report absences/dropouts explicitly.
2. Residents with a self-reported successful purchase / residents recruited; repeats on distinct days and how they were prompted. Clicks are not visits.
3. Correct current-claim audits / completed audits; also show unknown / attempted audits and every reported failed trip.
4. Total recurring operator minutes / active vendor-days; report onboarding separately. Do not compare this denominator directly with the larger pilot's vendor-week threshold.

No validated pass threshold is introduced for this small rehearsal. Missing recruitment/follow-up means insufficient evidence. Repeated inaccurate claims require pausing current-status publication and correcting the workflow. Use evidence to freeze the larger pilot plan before collecting its outcomes.

## Implementation response after evidence

If vendors struggle to update, simplify the observed stumbling point (session duration, item stock or wording). If residents cannot interpret freshness, fix that first. Defer AI summaries, GPS tracking, monetisation claims and regional expansion until supported. The full original vision remains unchanged.
