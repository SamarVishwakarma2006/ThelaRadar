# ThelaRadar — Manual pilot workflow

Prepared: 2026-11-07
Status: Step 8 preparation only. No participants recruited, directory published, messages sent or experiment run.

## Start conditions

Before launch, finish [locality selection](../research/pilot-area.md), conduct and synthesise [interviews](../research/interview-guide.md), and freeze the [pilot plan](pilot-plan.md). Record the operator, start/end dates, consent process, audit rota and tool access. Preparation can proceed while those tasks are open; outcome collection cannot be represented as completed.

## Minimal setup

Use one owner-controlled shared directory and a private operational register. Choose existing tools that the operator and participants can use; no paid service or account setup is required by this document. A view-only hosted document plus an agreed vendor update channel can be enough.

Keep the public directory separate from internal participant IDs, follow-up records and contact information. Participants should not have to join a group that exposes their phone numbers to strangers. Confirm a readable mobile experience and control who can edit. Only publish business details and photos with permission. Restrict the directory to the selected area and show that it is a pilot, not comprehensive coverage.

This repository stores instructions, empty templates and anonymised reports. Do not put a live directory of personal information or raw contact/consent records in Git.

## Directory card

Each permitted vendor card contains: public business name; selling landmark/location; dated menu/prices where known; usual schedule; status; confirmation source/time; expiry time; sold-out information; directions link; last operator refresh time; correction path.

Use 'Unknown' for missing values. Schedule-only information must not display as confirmed. A sighting proves neither item stock nor future attendance. If the chosen shared tool cannot update stale badges automatically, show explicit expiry times and refresh manually; customers must be told to treat expired evidence as unconfirmed.

## Provisional session rules — freeze before launch

For the recurring-location food pilot, propose a maximum two-hour confirmation lifetime, ending earlier at the vendor's declared finishing time. This is a trial operating rule, not a validated optimum. Adjust using interviews, then record the final value in the frozen plan. Do not apply it to future moving vendors by default.

A new valid vendor confirmation refreshes the session. 'Finished' ends it immediately; 'sold out' must distinguish the relevant item from the whole stall. No update by expiry changes current status to unconfirmed, not closed. Never infer availability from usual hours.

If operator coverage cannot keep the directory safe to interpret, pause current-availability claims and leave schedule-only listings. Record the resulting operating constraint.

## Vendor onboarding and updates

1. Explain the experiment, voluntary visibility and removal controls. Obtain agreement privately.
2. Assign an internal vendor ID and record the agreed selling sessions for the pilot.
3. Confirm permission to publish the card and demonstrate how to correct it.
4. Agree a familiar update channel and a short message format: 'Here at [selling landmark] until [time]', '[item] sold out', 'Finished', or 'Not coming today'.
5. Demonstrate one update without treating the demonstration as independent recurring adoption.

Log receipt and publication times separately, whether the update was self-initiated or prompted, and operator minutes. Validate ambiguous messages before publishing. Avoid individual daily chasing as an invisible subsidy; if it is necessary, record it.

## Daily operator routine

- Review consented updates and upcoming declared sessions; publish current evidence with timestamps.
- Check expiring confirmations during service windows and remove stale current-status claims.
- Complete preplanned rotating audits across vendors/dayparts, logging uncertain and failed checks too.
- Handle corrections and conflicting reports; preserve the original record internally and avoid blindly overriding a vendor.
- Collect optional purchase follow-up without tracking customers continuously.
- Record support, reminders, moderation and audit time, plus expenses and incidents.

Consumers can browse, inspect evidence and open directions. Ask only participants who agreed to follow-up whether they bought the item and whether the information helped. Avoid repeated leading prompts.

## Incident handling

Wrong location or false serving claim: correct or downgrade promptly, log the original claim and investigate. Item unavailable: distinguish stock from stall presence. Duplicate name: compare photo/landmark and verify before merging. Unresolved conflict: display uncertainty. Removal request: stop publication/sharing and handle records according to consent. Do not expose home/off-duty routes or infer food safety from reviews.

## Record keeping

Use the [pilot record templates](pilot-record-templates.md) for the field definitions and weekly report. Store identifiable operational logs in restricted storage outside Git. Publish only genuinely anonymised findings and aggregated counts.

At the end of each week, compare actual measures with the frozen plan, record missing data and all manual effort. Do not change thresholds retrospectively to make the pilot pass. At the end of four weeks, prepare evidence for Step 9; no automatic approval to build or expand follows.
