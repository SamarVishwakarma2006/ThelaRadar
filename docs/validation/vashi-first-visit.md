# Vashi — first field visit

Approved 9 November 2026. Owner-led reconnaissance; not a live service launch.

## Goal

Identify up to five recurring-location food stalls on one walkable route near Vashi station and learn how their availability actually changes. Record refusals and uncertainty. Five conversations is a target, not a claimed result.

## Before walking

- Pick a convenient visit time; avoid interrupting rush-hour sales.
- On arrival note the station exit, first/last landmark and approximate walking distance (about 500 metres total reach from the station).
- Use anonymous vendor codes V01–V05 in shared notes. Keep consent/contact records in ignored private/ or your private notebook.

## Introduction

“Hi, I’m Samar. I’m testing ThelaRadar, an idea to help people find nearby stalls and know their timings. Could I ask a few questions when you’re free? Nothing will be published without your permission, and I’m not promising customers.”

## Ask, then observe

1. What days/times were you actually here last week? What caused changes?
2. When did someone last arrive after you had closed or sold out?
3. How do regular customers currently find out you are here?
4. Would a short message such as ‘here until 8’, ‘poha sold out’ or ‘finished’ fit your routine? Which channel do you already use?
5. If willing, demonstrate one fictional update and time the task. Record help needed. This demonstration is not an unprompted update.
6. Would you join a small voluntary trial? Separately ask what business information/location may be shown, to whom, and how they can withdraw. Agreement to talk is not publication consent.

Do not request identity documents or publish phone numbers/home locations. Note the public selling landmark only with permission for subsequent publication. If someone declines, thank them and move on.

## Bring back

The selected exit/walking route, actual number approached and spoken to, number interested (not enrolled unless consent is complete), top three objections and anonymous examples. No names or contact details needed in chat or Git.

Then decide whether to recruit the seven-day rehearsal, change the update workflow or investigate another route. Keep the existing four-week pilot criteria unchanged; no real-world success is claimed from this visit alone.
