# ThelaRadar — Pilot region and audience

Updated: 2026-11-09
Status: Vashi station area, Navi Mumbai, approved by the owner for the first field rehearsal. Exact walking boundary and recruitment access require a first visit.

## Founder decision

Launch and validate ThelaRadar only in Maharashtra for now. Expansion beyond Maharashtra, including Delhi, is deferred until the local model is proven and a separate expansion decision is made.

This sets the geographic boundary; it is not a commitment to launch statewide at once. Start with one compact, reachable neighbourhood, then consider adjacent areas based on evidence. The original India-wide vision remains preserved.

Vashi station, Navi Mumbai, within roughly 500 metres of walking distance. ## Candidate locations

The founder suggested Mumbai (including Bandra), Navi Mumbai, Kalyan, Karjat, Mira Road and Panvel. Vashi station area has now been selected for the first rehearsal. Earlier recommendations to investigate Navi Mumbai, Kalyan and Panvel were provisional, not field-validated rankings.

Select the first locality using:

- Ability to visit several times a week and maintain vendor relationships.
- Access to residents and vendors willing to participate.
- Enough relevant vendors within a practical walking area.
- Evidence of recurring customer needs and gaps in current alternatives.
- Manageable onboarding, verification and support effort.

Do not pick a locality solely for popularity or theoretical market size.

## Proposed audience and category

Initial consumer hypotheses: students, PG residents, nearby workers and newcomers seeking affordable food; existing residents following favourite vendors may support repeat use.

Initial supply hypothesis: food stalls and carts at recurring selling locations, with vendors willing to maintain brief availability updates. Specific food categories, buying occasion and participant numbers remain to be set. Validate these hypotheses through interviews.

## Expansion sequence

1. One neighbourhood in Maharashtra.
2. Adjacent areas where reliable coverage and repeat usage can be maintained.
3. Additional Maharashtra cities, conditional on operating capacity and economics.
4. Other states only after an explicit evidence-backed expansion decision.

Moving vendors, visiting services, weekly markets and AI summaries remain in the long-term feature vision. This geographic decision does not accelerate their implementation or remove their validation requirements.
