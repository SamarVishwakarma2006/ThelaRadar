# ThelaRadar — Competitors and alternatives

Research date: 2026-11-06 (India time)
Status: Step 4 desk research complete. Local competitor coverage and customer switching behaviour remain untested.

## Finding

Street-vendor maps, real-time location, menus and availability alerts already appear in competing products' published descriptions. ThelaRadar cannot claim these features as unique. Its proposed opportunity is dependable coverage in a specific neighbourhood, transparent freshness and a low-effort vendor update process. This is a hypothesis to test, not a proven market gap.

## Method and evidence limits

Reviewed public first-party product pages, a developer-maintained app-store listing and Google help documentation. These establish what products advertise, not whether features work reliably or users adopt them. No account was created, app installed, customer interviewed or live vendor journey completed. This is a targeted comparison, not an exhaustive market census.

Do not infer active users, revenue, profitability or national coverage from a website, download count, promotional counter or app listing. A feature not mentioned in these materials remains unknown; it is not necessarily absent. Sources can change and should be rechecked before investment or public comparative claims.

## Direct competitors

| Product | Published offering | Overlap with ThelaRadar | Unknown / follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FollowMyBusiness | Live vendor GPS, nearby search, WhatsApp-number registration, menus, photos and directions [S1] | Moving-vendor discovery and vendor-managed listings | Actual local coverage, freshness, review functionality, ongoing pricing and vendor effort |
| Pahia | Dynamic vendor location; search by name/category/product/service; map/list views [S2] | Broad vendor discovery and tracking | Active supply in the chosen locality, accuracy, customer retention, schedule/stock handling and ongoing support |
| SpotOn | Location-verified activation, temporary sessions up to four hours, category discovery and favourite-vendor alerts [S3] | Availability, temporary vendors, following and local commerce | Whether device presence correlates with serving/stock; expiry behaviour in practice; local coverage, economics and retention |

SpotOn's published description overlaps with the proposed freshness/availability positioning. ThelaRadar must demonstrate execution and outcomes rather than claim that competitors only provide static pins. Device location alone is not independent proof that an item can be purchased.

## Other lead to investigate

SnackMap's indexed first-party material advertises a street-food map, menus, live status and a browser-based experience [S4]. Direct page extraction returned no readable content during this review. Treat this as a lower-confidence lead and inspect the working experience before relying on details, prices or coverage. It is not included as a validated feature comparison.

## Existing alternatives and switching hypotheses

| Alternative | Why someone might prefer it (hypothesis) | Question ThelaRadar must answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Maps | Familiar search/navigation and existing place information | Can better vendor coverage and recent evidence prevent more failed visits? |
| Instagram food content | Visual inspiration and creator recommendations | Can discovery lead to an available, affordable and suitable nearby purchase? |
| Local WhatsApp or resident groups | Known community and quick questions | Can structured information reduce repeated questions and stale answers? |
| Calling a known vendor | Direct clarification | Does the service help with unknown vendors or reduce repeated calling? |
| Asking neighbours or walking a familiar route | Existing habits requiring no new app | Is the improvement meaningful enough to change behaviour? |

These preference explanations are analyst hypotheses, not interview findings. Google explicitly supports contributions for missing places and corrections [S5]; it is inaccurate to claim that only popular or established places can appear there. The original Google premise remains a historical statement, not a product marketing claim.

## Positioning to test

For people buying affordable food in one compact neighbourhood, ThelaRadar helps identify relevant vendors and see when availability was last confirmed, with dated menus and practical directions.

Potential advantages to measure:

1. Dense relevant coverage within walking distance.
2. Clear distinction between usual hours, vendor confirmation and customer sightings.
3. Accurate end-of-session and sold-out handling.
4. Vendor updates that continue without daily individual chasing.
5. Repeat use for favourites and useful updates after initial discovery.

These are proposed performance advantages, not exclusive features or an established moat. AI summaries, map access and the app name do not establish defensibility.

## Local comparison protocol for Steps 5–8

After selecting the pilot area, compare the same permitted sample of vendors and customer tasks across ThelaRadar's manual service and available alternatives.

- Fix the sample and observation windows before comparing results; do not select only favourable examples.
- Record vendor coverage, menu/price recency, source/timestamp visibility and practical access details.
- Check whether each vendor is actually serving and whether the requested item is available, without publishing private information.
- Separate missing listings, unknown status, incorrect claims and unavailable features.
- Ask customers how they solved their most recent comparable need and observe repeat behaviour.
- Measure failed trips, time to a useful answer, vendor maintenance effort and the reason a customer chose an alternative.
- Report sample size and observation dates. Do not claim broad superiority from a small neighbourhood experiment.

Set quantitative success/stop thresholds in Step 7 before results are collected. If existing alternatives already solve the problem well enough, revise the scope rather than adding features automatically.

## Implications for the roadmap

Keep the recurring-location food pilot. Avoid a generic 'first street-vendor tracker' claim. Keep moving vendors, weekly markets, visiting services and AI in the original vision, but validate each separately. Do not commit to building until the Step 9 evidence review.
