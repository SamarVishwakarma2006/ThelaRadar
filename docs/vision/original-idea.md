# Original idea — Local Vendor

Status: Step 1 of 20 complete — original vision documented; demand and business assumptions unvalidated.
Recorded: 2026-11-06
Working project name: Local Vendor

## Origin and personal problem

The founder has struggled to find nearby places to eat because the location, operating times and menu of local stalls are not always known. Local knowledge is often shared through word of mouth or food videos, which may not answer whether a vendor is nearby, available today or suited to the customer's preferences.

## Original proposal (verbatim)

> IDEA - There is a problem with google maps. in india, we can only see popular restraunts and bars, and well established place. what if we had an app which locates local rediwala for multiple things like foods and snacks, utilities which comes once or twice a week. or weekly markets. Can use google maps to locate the place and the directions and thorugh user input and vendors input we can get the place and the timings of the vendor so it will be useful for the people to get that. It will also show the vendors which are moving through the cities, so people will be ready when the vendor comes. IT will be like a word of mouth for locals and people who are new to places and have to explore. It is somewhat like when you see an underated food stall on instagram. But the chances is that may not to be your liking, so there will be a basic review system, with ai overview, so people can get an overall idea of the place. This is a thing that i have also faced as i may not be able to find places that i want to eat from as i dont know the timing and the menu.

The Google Maps statement above preserves the founder's original wording and perception. It is not a verified claim that Google only lists established businesses. The opportunity to investigate is incomplete coverage and freshness for informal, temporary and moving vendors.

## Full original vision

A local discovery app for India that helps residents and newcomers find street vendors, visiting sellers or service providers, and weekly markets.

The full vision includes:

1. Nearby discovery of food stalls, snack carts, everyday goods and visiting utility/service vendors.
2. Locations, usual operating times, recurring schedules and weekly-market details.
3. Menus, products and prices where available.
4. Vendor-submitted information and community contributions, including missing listings and sightings.
5. Google Maps directions to the relevant selling location.
6. Moving-vendor visibility and useful arrival or stop updates so customers can prepare.
7. Local recommendations and basic customer reviews that help people assess suitability.
8. AI overviews of reviews, subject to sufficient reliable evidence.

These remain part of the long-term vision. Documenting them does not commit to shipping them all in the first release.

## Intended people and value

- Residents: find vendors and avoid missing familiar sellers.
- Newcomers: learn about affordable local options without already having local connections.
- Vendors: become discoverable and communicate their location, schedule and offerings.
- Community contributors: share useful neighbourhood knowledge and corrections.

These are proposed audiences, not validated customer segments. The paying customer is still undecided.

## Core problem to investigate

Can people reliably find the nearby vendor or item they want, at the time they need it, with enough information to decide whether to visit?

## Assumptions requiring evidence

- Enough people experience this problem repeatedly and lack a satisfactory alternative.
- Customers will act on the information and return after discovering a few favourites.
- Vendors want additional visibility and can maintain useful updates during selling hours.
- Community contributions can improve coverage without creating excessive errors or abuse.
- Availability, menus and locations can stay accurate at a sustainable operating cost.
- Moving vendors will voluntarily share business locations or announce useful stops.
- Weekly markets and utility vendors benefit from the same product or suitable variations.
- Reviews will contain enough independent, recent information for useful summaries.
- A payer and sustainable revenue model can be established.

## Boundaries and unresolved questions

No pilot area, launch category, technology stack, budget, revenue model or valuation has been selected. No customer interviews, vendor participation, traction or financial forecasts are claimed in this document. Existing competitors mean novelty must not be assumed.
